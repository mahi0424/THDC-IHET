The Festival of Lights 2026

Diwali, or Deepavali, is one of the most significant festivals in Indian culture. In 2026, the main day of celebrations falls on Sunday, November 8th. It marks the victory of righteousness and the lifting of spiritual darkness.

Dhanteras

Nov 6, 2026: The festival begins with the worship of wealth and prosperity.

Nov 8, 2026: The main event where families gather to pray to Goddess Lakshmi.

Bhai Dooj

Nov 10, 2026: Celebrating the sacred bond between brothers and sisters.
